How it works

Three stages, one ticket.

Stage 1

Set your price

Pick any product on the store and name the price that makes it a deal for you. Your dibs ticket opens instantly.

Stage 2

We keep watch

Deal Dibs checks the listed price continuously in the background, so you don't have to think about it again.

Stage 3

It's claimed

The moment the price meets your target, the order goes through automatically and you get a confirmation.

Why it works

Built for shoppers who wait on purpose.

01 Real-time monitoring

Prices are checked continuously, not on a slow daily cron — so a flash sale doesn't slip past your ticket.

02 Auto-checkout on trigger

When the target is hit, the order is placed using the shopper's saved payment details — no last-minute scramble.

03 Multiple tickets at once

Shoppers can hold dibs on as many products as they like, each watched independently.

04 Merchant-side controls

Store owners set floors, limits, and eligible products, so automated orders stay within their rules.
